A little about me

An engineer
by curiosity.

Engineering begins with a better question.

Based in Sylhet, Bangladesh, I build projects at the intersection of robotics, embedded hardware, and software. My work ranges from sensor-driven IoT prototypes to mobile rovers, alongside research editing and hands-on STEM workshops. I value clear documentation, iterative testing, and engineering that serves real needs.
